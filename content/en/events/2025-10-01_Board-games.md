Every Friday we meet at ETH Zurich for board games and a social evening. Anyone in Zurich is welcome — members, students, alumni, and guests. You do not need to join the association first.

We speak a mix of Russian and English; you do not need to be fluent in Russian. See the calendar on this site for the next date and room.
